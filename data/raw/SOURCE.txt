BROKEN WORDS — 20 instances across 13 articles
  Words split by a space that does not belong: "عشر ين" (عشرين), "تو افر"
  (توافر), "مض ي" (مضي), "نشر ها" (نشرها), "المع الجة" (المعالجة), and 15
  more. Detected by: the joined form occurs elsewhere in the same corpus as a
  standalone token.

  ADR-013 records "0 remaining" for this defect class. That claim is FALSE.
  Origin is NOT settled: pdfplumber's independent word-splitting reproduces
  some of these breaks (they are in the document) and not others (ours). Two
  probes disagreed, and naive visual-order reversal is unreliable across
  Arabic ligatures, so no per-word attribution is published here. What is
  established: the breaks exist, and they depress lexical retrieval, because
  BM25 indexes "عشر" and "ين" as two tokens.

OCR — THE RE-INGEST PATH IS BLOCKED, MEASURED (ADR-020)
  Re-ingesting from the gazette needs an OCR engine that reads the DIGITS.
  Scored by `python tasks.py ocr-gate` against hand-read ground truth
  (evals/ocr/digit_ground_truth.json — 42 digit tokens on pages 7, 10 and 28,
  read off the images by eye; an engine cannot be its own ruler):

      easyocr 1.7.2                                 71.4%   0/3 crit  FAIL
      PaddleOCR 3.7.0  server_det + arabic v5 rec   21.4%   0/3 crit  FAIL
      PaddleOCR 3.7.0  mobile_det + arabic v5 rec   16.7%   0/3 crit  FAIL
      surya-ocr 0.17.1 torch/CPU                    92.9%   3/3 crit  FAIL

  easyocr CORRUPTS: المادة (١٢) -> ١٢١, and ٣٦، -> ٣٦١ (it reads the Arabic
  comma as a digit). Silent, well-formed, and it invents articles.

  Paddle DELETES: مادة (٤) -> مادة. Its Arabic prose is cleaner than
  easyocr's, but with no article numbers there is nothing to segment. Proven
  to be the recognition model, not detection: a hand-cropped header line
  carrying ٢٨/١٥/٢٠٢٠/٢٩ came back as "لجريدة الرسميالعدد مكرر فىولية سنة"
  — words intact, all four numbers gone, with no detection stage involved.

  surya is the closest by a wide margin and the only one that gets every
  critical case right. Pages 7 and 10 came back 100%; all three errors are on
  page 28 — a margin page number and the tail of one cross-reference list.
  Every article header was correct, and article headers are what segments the
  corpus. It ALSO reproduced every fidelity finding independently, from a
  completely different architecture than easyocr: «اثنتين» twice, «بموافاة»,
  «لصالح», «الإلكترونية», «بالكلمات والعبارات», zero crossover. Third witness.

  Two caveats on surya. It is not a verbatim transcription — it normalises
  alef maqsura to ya (فى -> في); search_normalize folds that away so retrieval
  cannot see it, but evaluation_normalize can. And it emits HTML tags, so it
  cannot feed ingest directly: src/legalrag/ocr_text.py strips markup, drops
  the running header and page numbers, and orders pages numerically.

  It runs in a separate conda env (Python 3.12): surya pins pillow<11, whose
  last release predates cp314 wheels, so installing it beside the project
  would downgrade Pillow out from under easyocr and sentence-transformers.
  Version 0.22 also dropped the torch backend entirely (vllm-in-Docker /
  llama.cpp / OpenAI client only), which is why 0.17.1 is the one measured.

  FULL GAZETTE RUN, MEASURED, NOT ADOPTED (ADR-021)
  surya was run over all 29 pages and ingested in an ISOLATED copy — the
  shipped corpus was never touched. It parsed into 56 articles, 7 issuance +
  49 law: the published structure exactly. Article by article against the
  shipped corpus: mean similarity 0.988 under search_normalize (what retrieval
  sees), 0.973 verbatim, 55 of 56 at or above 0.90. Three article-header
  numbers were misread (38->28, 42->21, 44->144), every one of them detectable
  from its position in the sequence.

  The single article below 0.90 is issuance-7, and the gap favours the gazette:
  it carries the presidential attestation block that the republication dropped.
  A second broken word was settled the same way — the shipped corpus has
  `مض ي` where the gazette has `مضى` whole.

  Mohamed's decision: do NOT replace the corpus. Correcting misread numbers
  from position is a human hand on the text of record, and at 0.988 the
  retrieval numbers would barely move — what the intervention buys is a
  stronger CLAIM, not a better measurement. Kept as evidence at
  data/processed/gazette_ocr_surya.json and gazette_surya_raw.txt (derived,
  gitignored).

  SO THIS STILL HOLDS: the Q001/Q013 keyword correction stays unapplied, and
  no number in EVAL.md may be restated as a claim about Egyptian law 151/2020.

EXTRACTION (ADR-013)
  This PDF stores Arabic in visual order; pdfplumber's own extract_text()
  returns every word reversed. src/legalrag/pdf_text.py reconstructs logical
  order. Four defects were found and fixed while doing so — see ADR-013. The
  one to remember: Arabic-Indic digits (٠-٩) sit inside the Arabic Unicode
  block, so a naive reversal turned مادة (٢٥) into مادة (٥٢).
